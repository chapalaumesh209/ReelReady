# ReelReady

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Implementation choice under the user's instruction to build from scratch: React with the Sites Vinext starter; server-side generation; Supabase Postgres drafts and private resumable video storage, with browser caching. No ReelReady account or sign-in; explicit social authorization at publishing. User prioritizes a simple, reviewable first version.

## Users

Solo and team-supported social media creators, especially multilingual Indian creators.

## Product Purpose

One connected workflow per reel: plan ideas, schedule, platforms and languages; record and upload the video; review and revise its English script and captions by voice; create an English audio version; publish or schedule to connected platforms through Upload-Post.

## Operating Context

Installed Wispr Flow dictates into ordinary editable fields. Desktop Command Mode can refine selected text. Typing and examples work without Wispr.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Before recording: hook, outline, shot checklist. After recording: Instagram caption/hashtags, Shorts title/description, X post. Language and script separate. Optional caption examples and brand details. Editable results, copy, character counts, validation, loading, retry and private cloud persistence tied to an anonymous browser session. No ReelReady login. Each visitor must explicitly connect their own social accounts through Upload-Post before publishing. Keep planning, video and publishing data together. No fabricated analytics or publishing-success claims. Server secrets only. No invented claims. English, Telugu and Hindi first. No app-imposed video file-size cap; provider/project and destination limits remain.

## Brand Commitments

ReelReady. Original voice-wave identity inspired by Wispr Flow. Smooth interface. From your voice to your next reel.

## Evidence on Hand

User's detailed accepted specifications in this chat. No verified time-saving benchmark. Sample content must be labelled. Google Gemini generation is connected server-side. Output is schema-validated, checked for platform length and script, and repaired once when needed. Creator-provided links and disclosure text are preserved.

## Product Principles

Fast first result; no mandatory setup; preserve meaning; review before copying; no repeated entry.

## Current workflow decision
The user explicitly replaced the independent before/after modes with a sequential plan → record/upload → review/dub → publish flow. They requested actual English video audio, not only translated captions, and selected Upload-Post for publishing. Standard synthetic English voiceover over original visuals is the first dubbing implementation; no voice cloning or lip-sync claim. Publishing credential is pending owner configuration.
