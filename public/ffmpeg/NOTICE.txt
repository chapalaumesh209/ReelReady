ReelReady uses unmodified upstream FFmpeg.wasm binaries.

Wrapper: @ffmpeg/ffmpeg 0.12.15 (MIT)
Source: https://github.com/ffmpegwasm/ffmpeg.wasm/tree/main/packages/ffmpeg

Core: @ffmpeg/core 0.12.10 (GPL-2.0-or-later)
The distributed ffmpeg-core.js and concatenation of core-0.bin + core-1.bin
are the unmodified upstream single-threaded core, split solely for hosting.
